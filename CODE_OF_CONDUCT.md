# Code of conduct

This project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text at that address applies to every project space: the repository,
its issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and anywhere a person is
representing the project. What follows is a summary in our own words; where the
two differ, the full text is what counts.

## Our pledge

Everyone who takes part in this project, as a contributor or as a maintainer,
pledges to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone,
whatever their background, identity, experience or circumstances.

## Our standards

What we expect:

- Be respectful and considerate, and assume good faith.
- Give feedback about the work, not the person, and take feedback gracefully.
- Own your mistakes, apologise to those affected and learn from them.
- Put the health of the community ahead of winning an argument.

What is not acceptable:

- Harassment in any form, in public or in private.
- Insults, personal or political attacks, and trolling.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaks this code, tell the
maintainers privately, through GitHub. There are two ways:

- Use the repository's
  [private reporting form](https://github.com/zd87pl/private-ai-adapters/security/advisories/new).
  It is the form used for security reports and it reaches only the maintainers;
  begin your message by saying that it is a conduct report.
- Contact a maintainer directly on GitHub.

Please do not use a public issue for a conduct report.

## Enforcement

The maintainers are responsible for enforcing this code. They will review every
report, treat it confidentially and protect the privacy of the person who made
it. They follow the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant, which
range from a private correction to a permanent ban, depending on how serious and
how repeated the behaviour is.
